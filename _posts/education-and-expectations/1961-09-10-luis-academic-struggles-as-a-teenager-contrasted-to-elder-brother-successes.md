<h1>Luis' Academic Struggles as a Teenager Contrasted to Elder Brother Successes</h1>
<p>September 10th, 1961<br>
Original letter sent</p>

<p>My mother saved around 200 of my handwritten letters sent to my parents beginning in 1959 when I was sent from Brazil to a British boarding school in St. George's College in Quilmes, Buenos Aires, Argentina to the end of 1970. Before she passed away, she sent them to me with a note that read: My advice is to keep these letters until you have plenty of time to go through them slowly and appreciate them. They refresh so memories. She was right as she usually was.</p>

<p>In one of those letters to my parents sent on September 10th, 1961, as a fifteen-year-old on occasion of their visit to England to settle my oldest brother, John, at Cambridge University, immediately after celebrating his achievement I noted that I came ante-cola/before last place in my class.</p>

<p>September 10, 1961</p>

<p>Dear Mum and Dad,</p>

<p>Here I am again. Well I was thinking of you yesterday on the Aragon and the excitement and at the same time the disappointment of leaving jolly old England and Pon (my brother John) and Deets, (my brother Derek) any way you must have pleased to see them so settled own and Pon in Cambridge. Isn't it terrific? I came ante-cola in the form order (before last in my class) but I beg you Dad and Mum to take no heed to it. I am at present enjoying every second of my life here, I am really settled in.</p>
